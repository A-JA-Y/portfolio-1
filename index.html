About me

1+ yrs In the timeline

I’m Saurav — a video editor and motion designer with 1+ years of turning raw footage into stories that hold attention and convert.

My day lives inside Premiere Pro and After Effects, with Photoshop and Figma close by. I cut SaaS launch videos, real estate films, commercial ads and social-media content — always pacing the edit around the story first, the trend second.

Whether it’s a 6-second hook or a 3-minute brand film, I handle the full pass: structure, motion graphics, grade, sound and delivery.

I’ve cut for Edit Tree, Meritshot and InfoEdge in Noida, and I’m currently editing remotely for AI Certs (US).
